Braille Project

Our project aims to bring technology closer to people with visual impairments through a physical keyboard based on the Braille system. With this proposal we aim to foster inclusion, facilitate communication, and promote equal opportunities in education and technology.

The project

What does our project involve?

The project consists of developing a physical keyboard inspired by the Braille system. Its purpose is to allow people with visual impairments to write and use technological devices in a simpler, more intuitive, and more independent way.

Three pillars

What holds the project together

Goal

Develop an accessible tool that allows people with visual impairments to interact with computers and other devices in a more comfortable and independent way.

Braille Keyboard

The keyboard uses six buttons that represent the six dots of the Braille cell. Each combination allows writing letters, numbers, and symbols easily.

Inclusion

Our project seeks to remove technological barriers and promote equal opportunities through accessible solutions for everyone.

A letter's journey

How does it work?

1

Press

The user presses a combination of the six buttons on the Braille keyboard.

2

Process

The microcontroller identifies the combination and converts it into a letter or symbol.

3

Send

The information is automatically sent to the computer or connected device.

4

Write

The character appears on screen, enabling fast, accessible typing.
